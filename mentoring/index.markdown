## What I Can Help With

I can help with questions and challenges such as creating a learning plan, understanding difficult topics, resolving errors, pair programming or collaborative development, and finding projects through crowdsourcing platforms.

- Creating a programming learning plan
- Questions and error resolution during programming study
- Pair programming and collaborative development
- Advice on finding projects through crowdsourcing platforms
- Career-change consultations

Engineers are also expected to develop the ability to research and solve errors and other challenges independently. I also welcome questions from people who have researched an issue themselves but are unsure how to proceed.
